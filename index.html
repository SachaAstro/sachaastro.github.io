Sacha Peters

I'm a PhD student in AI for weather forecasting at the University of Liège, supervised by Gilles Louppe and Xavier Fettweis.

I'm building MAR.ia, a diffusion-based emulator of the regional climate model MAR. It turns coarse ERA5 data into high-resolution surface fields over Belgium, and I use it to generate fast weather forecast ensembles. Before that, I worked on deep learning for gravitational wave detectors.

Research

MAR.ia: a diffusion-based emulator for high-resolution climate downscaling over Belgium
Sacha Peters, Elise Faulx
Work in progress, 2026

A diffusion model maps 6-hourly ERA5 (0.25°) to hourly MAR fields at 5 km. Sampling is fast, so we get ensembles to measure uncertainty. Next steps: extreme events, and all MAR variables.
